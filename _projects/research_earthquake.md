Development of low-cost methods for monitoring and early warning for geo-hazards.

- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. Tahmeed Malik Al-Hussaini, Professor, Civil Engineering (CE), BUET
- **Co-Principal Investigator:** Dr. Md Zunaid Baten, Associate Professor, EEE, BUET
- **Funded by:** Research and Innovation Center for Science and Engineering (RISE) Internal Research Grant, BUET

Conducted noise analysis of MEMS sensors, determining the optimal device for signals down to 0.1 m/s². Built a stand-alone unit, validated it in the lab, and field-deployed a multi-station network that detected multiple earthquakes.
